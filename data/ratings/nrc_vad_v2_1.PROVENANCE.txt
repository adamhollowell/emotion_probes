Local transformation:
  Extracted the archive; took the combined Unigrams file
  Unigrams/unigrams-NRC-VAD-Lexicon-v2.1.txt (44,728 unigrams; tab-separated with
  header term,valence,arousal,dominance) and canonicalized to
  data/ratings/nrc_vad_v2_1.csv with columns word,valence,arousal,dominance
  (word lowercased, one row per term). Conversion + use:
  scripts/analyze_sun_choi_engagement.py (#4 block).

Scale:
  v2.1 scores are real-valued on a roughly -1 (lowest) to +1 (highest) scale for each
  of valence, arousal, dominance. (Differs from v1's 0-1 range; immaterial here since
  all uses are correlations, which are scale-invariant.)

Terms of use / redistribution note:
  Free for non-commercial research and educational use with citation.
  Do NOT redistribute the raw data. Direct readers to the official NRC-VAD homepage.

Use in this project:
  P1.6 (Sun et al. 2026 engagement) norm-source robustness check: against Sun et al.'s
  exact lexicon (this 44,728-unigram set), our L31 global-171 PCA gives PC2<->arousal
  r = 0.636 over 161 covered emotions, best PC = PC2 -- ruling out norm source as the
  explanation for their arousal null. Also available for the P1.4 BLOG human-baseline
  stress test. Does NOT replace strict Essay-1 Figure 8, which remains
  Russell-Mehrabian 1977 only.

Integrity / release note:

  data/ratings/nrc_vad_v2_1.csv and data/ratings/NRC-VAD-Lexicon-v2.1/.
  Commit this provenance record and the analysis script only, not the raw lexicon.
