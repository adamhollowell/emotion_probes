Warriner et al. 2013 VAD norms — Local Provenance Record

Local filename:
  data/ratings/warriner_cache.csv   (verbatim local cache of the published supplementary
  file; 13,915 lemmas + header row; .gitignored — see release note below)

Original resource:
  Norms of Valence, Arousal, and Dominance for 13,915 English lemmas
  (the "Warriner norms"; Amazon Mechanical Turk ratings, 1-9 Likert scales).

Creators:
  Amy Beth Warriner, Victor Kuperman, and Marc Brysbaert.

Official homepage / source:
  Center for Reading Research (CRR), Ghent University — dataset archive:
  http://crr.ugent.be/archives/1003
  (Distributed as supplementary material to the Behavior Research Methods paper below.)

Paper citation:
  Warriner, A. B., Kuperman, V., & Brysbaert, M. (2013). "Norms of valence, arousal, and
  dominance for 13,915 English lemmas." Behavior Research Methods, 45(4), 1191-1207.
  doi:10.3758/s13428-012-0314-x

Scale:
  Valence, arousal, and dominance are each rated on a 1-9 scale (1 = most negative / calm /
  controlled; 9 = most positive / aroused / in-control). Immaterial to our use, which is
  correlational and therefore scale-invariant.

Columns used:
  The cache retains all original Warriner columns (per-word means with M/F, younger/older,
  and low/high sub-splits). This project reads only three:
    Word          — the lemma (join key)
    V.Mean.Sum    — mean valence over all raters
    A.Mean.Sum    — mean arousal over all raters

Local transformation:
  NONE. warriner_cache.csv is the unmodified published supplementary CSV (a local cache).
  Canonicalization to (word, valence, arousal) happens at read time inside
  scripts/analyze_sun_choi_engagement.py (#4 norm-source block), not on disk.

Use in this project:
  §9.3 / P1.6 (Sun et al. 2026 engagement) norm-source robustness check. Against the Warriner
  norms our L31 global-171 PCA gives, on the 126 overlapping emotions, PC1<->pleasure
  r = 0.687 and PC2<->arousal r = 0.477, with arousal's best component = PC2 — one of three
  human-norm sources (Russell-Mehrabian 0.62, Warriner 0.48, NRC-VAD 0.64) that all place
  arousal on PC2, showing the alignment is not an artifact of any single norm instrument.
  Warriner is the weakest of the three and is reported as such (it is not the main benchmark;
  Essay-1 Figure 8 remains Russell-Mehrabian 1977 only).
