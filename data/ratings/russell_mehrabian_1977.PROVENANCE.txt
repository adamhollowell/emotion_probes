PROVENANCE — russell_mehrabian_1977.csv
========================================

Source
------
Russell, J. A., & Mehrabian, A. (1977). Evidence for a three-factor theory
of emotions. Journal of Research in Personality, 11(3), 273–294.

This is the human-norm source used by Sofroniew et al. (2026,
arXiv:2604.07729) for their Figure 8 comparison (their reference [9]), and
therefore the exact benchmark inherited by this replication (Essay 1, §5.2
/ ledger P1.2).

Contents
--------
45 rows (+ header): the emotion terms from Russell & Mehrabian's rating
study that overlap the 171-emotion vocabulary of Sofroniew et al. (2026).
Columns: Term, N (raters), Pleasure Mean/SD, Arousal Mean/SD, Dominance
Mean/SD. Values are the published normative ratings on [-1, +1] scales.

Role in the pipeline
--------------------
Canonical input to the Figure 8 human-norm comparison
(repo/github_repo/scripts/render_parallel_fig8_russell_mehrabian.py) and the
layerwise affect-alignment sweep (layerwise_va_global.py). The PC1↔Pleasure
and PC2↔Arousal correlations reported in Essay 1 (r = 0.7203 / 0.6675 at L31
on the canonical basis, vectors sha256 6de2e293…) are computed against this table,
restricted to these 45 overlap emotions after a global PCA over all 171
vectors. This file is basis-independent; only the vectors change with basis.

Producer-name correction (2026-08-17)
-------------------------------------
Earlier revisions of this file and of the findings ledger named
"fig8_rebuild_locked_audit.py" as the Figure 8 producer. No such file has
ever existed in this repository — it is a documentation ghost. The
authoritative producer, and the one named by
locked_figures_and_tables/LOCK_RECORD.json, is
render_parallel_fig8_russell_mehrabian.py.

Earlier r figures quoted here were computed on superseded bases and no longer
apply; current values are computed against the canonical vectors (sha256 6de2e293).

Redistribution rationale
------------------------
A small derived table of factual normative ratings transcribed from a
published 1977 paper, redistributed with citation. It is not a licensed
lexicon; the licensed third-party lexicons used in robustness checks
(NRC-VAD v2.1, Warriner 2013) are NOT bundled with the release and ship as
provenance + fetch scripts only.

History (2026-07-11 consolidation, per ledger PROV.7(c))
--------------------------------------------------------
Three content-identical variants of this table existed, differing only in
header format (verified 315/315 cells identical). This file
(russell_mehrabian_1977.csv, "Capitalized Space" headers, read by the
locked Figure 8 producer) is the single canonical release file. The two
redundant variants — russell_mehrabian_45_emotions.csv and
russell_mehrabian_overlap_45.csv (read only by the retired v3 pipeline) —
were retired to archive_historical/ on 2026-07-11. A corrupted
transcription (russell_mehrabian_overlap_45_DISCARD_BAD.csv, 77 divergent
cells) was previously deleted and must never be used.
